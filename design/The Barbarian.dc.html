The complete study of El Sewedy Electric: backlog, copper, and what the export mix actually earns.
The Barbarian · 4 Aug · 14 min read
The verdict
{{ dialVerdict }}
Cheap on FY26 earnings, but only if the backlog converts on schedule.
El Sewedy has spent four years turning a domestic cable business into an export contractor. FY25 revenue of EGP 131.9bn is 61% above FY22, and roughly a third of it is now earned outside Egypt. That shift is the whole investment case, and it is also the reason the margin line keeps moving.
Export contracts carry the backlog, not the margin. Those are different businesses wearing the same ticker.
Strip out the Saudi transmission award and gross margin is flat year on year. The market is paying 7.4× FY26 for a company whose order book is now longer than its history of delivering one.
