00 / Start here

BIOC in
normal English.

A genuine pharmaceutical business, with a global parent standing behind it and a clean filing record, whose share price has been set by a tiny number of shares changing hands very fast.

  1. 01

    Almost none of it is actually for sale.

    The free float is 2.91% — 2,434,272 shares. Everything else is held by the parent and long-term holders. At EGP 194 the entire tradable pool is worth about EGP 472m, against a company valued at EGP 16.2bn.

    2.91%
  2. 02

    That pool turned over three and a half times in two weeks.

    In one single session, 45.8% of the entire free float traded. Across ten sessions the whole float changed hands 3.54 times. A price set that way is not a valuation — it is a queue.

    3.54× in 10 days
  3. 03

    An index put a buyer in that queue.

    BIOC was added to the EGX70 EWI and EGX100 EWI in the review announced 27 January 2026, effective 1 February. Index inclusion obliges funds to buy — into 2.4 million shares. That is mechanical, not a company event.

    Forced buyers
  4. 04

    The earnings did not follow.

    Q1 2026 net profit was EGP 4.7m. Not 4.7 billion — 4.7 million, against a EGP 16.2bn market value. Profit fell 92% and sales fell 18%.

    ≈102× earnings
  5. 05

    None of this is a criticism of the company.

    No distress was found anywhere in the accounts, the parent is a global pharmaceutical group, ownership filings are clean and unchanged, and the company answered the exchange promptly when asked. Those are the reasons two pillars score positive.

    A good company

01 / The pillar retail screens ignore

2,434,272 shares.
That is the whole market.

Tradability is the fifth pillar of this framework and it is the one that hurts most, because it is invisible on every screen a retail investor looks at. Price and volume are displayed everywhere. The size of the pool those trades came out of is displayed nowhere.

Why this scores −10 and not −7. The band for −5 to −10 in this framework reads: float so small the quoted price is not a price you can transact at scale. BIOC is the clearest example the series has found. A single institution deciding to build a EGP 500m position would have to buy more than the entire free float. The number on the screen is real for one share and fictional for one thousand.

And it cuts both ways. The same arithmetic that let the price rise on very little buying will let it fall on very little selling, and the EGX ±20% daily limit means the exit can simply close for a session at a time.

02 / The mechanical driver

Index inclusion
into almost no stock.

An index review is not news about a business. Nothing improved at BIOC on 27 January 2026. What changed is that a category of buyer became obliged to own it — and the pool they had to buy from was 2.4 million shares.

  1. 01

    The review, announced 27 January 2026.

    BIOC was added to the EGX70 EWI and the EGX100 EWI, effective 1 February 2026. Equal-weighted indices size positions without reference to how much stock exists.

    Two indices
  2. 02

    Forced demand meets a fixed supply.

    Tracking funds must buy on the effective date whatever the price. When the tradable supply is 2.91% of the company, the price does the adjusting.

    Mechanical
  3. 03

    It is a two-way door.

    EGX reviews these indices semi-annually — the next one announced at the end of July, effective August. Removal reverses the mechanism into the same 2.91% float. This is a structural note, not a prediction.

    Reviews cut both ways

Recorded as a structural fact, not an accusation. Index inclusion is a published, rules-based process. Nothing here suggests anyone did anything improper — only that a price moved for a reason that has nothing to do with the company's earnings, which is exactly the kind of thing this series exists to separate out.

03 / What you are paying for

EGP 16.2bn of value.
EGP 4.7m of quarterly profit.

The earnings themselves are honest. They are real operating pharmaceutical profits — no revaluation gains, no asset disposals, no accounting theatre. There are simply very few of them relative to the price.

  • Trailing earnings multipleAt EGP 194 ≈ 102×
  • Q1 2026 net profitAgainst a EGP 16.2bn market value EGP 4.7m−92% year on year
  • Q1 2026 salesDirection of the top line −18%

Earnings quality −4, not −9. The framework's automatic penalties are for one-off profits and for profit that never becomes cash. Neither applies here: this is operating pharma income. The deduction is for the sheer thinness of it — a quarterly profit of EGP 4.7m tells you almost nothing is reaching the bottom line right now.

Growth −9. A 92% fall in profit alongside an 18% fall in sales is the framework's clearest case of the latest quarter overriding everything else. Whatever the three-year record looks like, this is the direction of travel today.

04 / The verdict

Trash at 194.
And not the company's fault.

Two pillars are positive. Two are at or near the floor. That spread is the whole story: this framework scores a security at a price, and a security is more than the business behind it.

Valuation −10. EGP 194 is approximately 102× trailing earnings for a market capitalisation of roughly EGP 16.2bn. That is a price detached from any earnings anchor, which is what the bottom of the band describes.

Balance sheet +4. No distress was located anywhere in the accounts, and a global pharmaceutical parent stands behind the company. Held at +4 rather than higher because the balance sheet is not what is being priced here.

Governance +4 — genuinely positive. The company answered the exchange promptly on 19 July. Ownership filings are clean and unchanged. The only deduction is a minor listing-committee warning in June over a late filing.

What would flip it: a real free float. At 2.91%, the quoted price is not a price you can transact at scale in either direction, and no improvement in the business fixes that. A secondary offering, a parent sell-down, or any event that puts a meaningful number of shares into public hands would change this entry more than a good quarter would.
