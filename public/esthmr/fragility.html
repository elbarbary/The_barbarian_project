How Challenger C-v2 Works: Decoupled Multi-Channel Escalation

Standard frontier market crash models face a brutal dilemma: tuning for high recall causes constant false alarms, while tightening thresholds misses sudden exogenous shocks. Challenger C-v2 solves this trade-off by combining a 2-day domestic consensus gate with two decoupled exogenous fast-paths (Commodity Inflation and Global Volatility Contagion), bounded by an 8-session hold ceiling.

Pillar 01 · Domestic Consensus −25.8% False Days

2-Day Persistence Consensus Gate

Sv4[t] ≥ 0.930 && Sv4[t−1] ≥ 0.930
&& ΔS5 ≥ 0.000 && Stress_Groups ≥ 1

Domestic fragility must be verified across two consecutive market sessions with non-negative 5-day slope. This completely filters out single-session transient dips, block-trade liquidity ripples, and decelerating bull market consolidations.

Key Impact: False alert days drop from 608 to 451 sessions while maintaining 100% broad crisis recall.
Pillar 02 · Exogenous Fast-Path Immediate Bypass

Decoupled Commodity & Contagion Bypasses

Sv4 ≥ 0.90 && Stress ≥ 1 && (Ccomm ≥ 0.60 || Crisk ≥ 0.45)
Ccomm = max(Brent20d/25%, Wheat20d/25%)
Crisk = 0.40·ΔVIX5/8 + 0.20·(VIX−20)/20 + ...

Severe external shocks strike Egypt via two separate transmission channels: commodity import-bill inflation (oil/wheat drain on reserves) vs. global financial flight (VIX surge, dollar index spikes, MSCI EM selloffs). Decoupling them allows sudden crises (e.g. 2022 Ukraine, 2020 COVID) to trigger immediately at S ≥ 0.90 without waiting for a 2-day domestic lag.

Key Impact: Lifts strict early hits from 14/17 to 16/17 and advances median lead time to 17.5 sessions.
Pillar 03 · Execution Discipline Low Market Drag

Bounded Hold Duration & Cooldown

Min Hold = 5 days · Max Hold = 8 days
Refractory Cooldown = 20 days
Exit: Sv4 < 0.885 && Catalysts < 70% threshold

Caps maximum alert duration at 8 sessions (down from 12). In prolonged multi-month downturns, alerts do not drag indefinitely across consolidation recoveries. A 20-session refractory cooldown prevents redundant duplicate alerts following an episode.

Key Impact: Alert occupancy falls to 12.53%, leaving capital free to participate in 87.5% of market upside.
