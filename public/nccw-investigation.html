01 / Rule one of the series

One share in 2015
is 39.64 shares today.

Nine times the company distributed treasury shares as bonus shares — «توزيع أسهم خزينة كأسهم منحة». Issued capital never changed; the number of shares in your account did. Chain the factors together and the cumulative divisor is 39.64125.

  • What the screen showsEleven years, unadjusted price line −50%
  • What a holder actually madeSame period, adjusted for all nine distributions +1,864%19.6× the money
  • The same result in dollarsEstimate — labelled as one ≈ +220%

How the sanitizer finds it. Every EGX price sits on a 0.01 tick grid. When a vendor adjusts history, the adjusted values stop landing on that grid — so you search for the factor f that puts every open, high, low and close back on it. The factor changes exactly at each ex-date, which hands you the corporate-action calendar without needing the announcements. Eleven years of NCCW produce ten eras: 39.64125 → 36.03775 → 32.7615 → 21.841 → 12.6 → 2.52 → 1.68 → 1.5615 → 1.2 → 1.

The proof is the theoretical ex-price. On 6 August 2026 the cum close was EGP 7.12 and the factor 1.20. 7.12 ÷ 1.2 = 5.93, and the stock opened at exactly 5.93. Not approximately — exactly. Same test on 21 August 2025: 8.92 ÷ 1.30125 = 6.85, and it opened at 6.85.

The gap fills, and fast. Having opened at the mechanically correct 5.93 on 6 August, the stock closed at 6.38 — retracing 99% of the drop intraday. The 2016 distribution did the same thing: 98% intraday, 94% at the close, on 7.4× volume. The market treats the free shares as free money, every time.

02 / The number that breaks the bull case

The parent is building
EGP 15.59bn of work.
NCCW got none of it.

The whole bull case for NCCW is that its 52%-owner, Madinet Masr, is in a construction boom and must feed its own contractor. That is a reasonable thing to assume. It is also checkable — and Madinet Masr quantifies it in its own related-party note.

Q1 2026NIL0.0%
MeanwhileEGP 15.59bn of remaining contractor commitments, and EGP 8.5bn of MoUs signed with third-party contractors

There is no framework agreement and no preferred-contractor arrangement. Nothing in either company's filings obliges the parent to award work to its own subsidiary, and the trend of what it actually awards runs to zero.

Which is what makes the August award genuinely interesting. The EGP 462,799,043 Origami–Sarai contract is real and it is large — 8.7× everything the parent gave NCCW in all of FY2025. It is also the first award after a quarter of nothing, and it carries no related-party label in the disclosure. One award does not reverse a two-year trend; a second one, on disclosed terms, would start to.

03 / The rights issue, done properly

Not dilution.
A cash call.

201,600,000 new shares at EGP 1.00 against 252,000,000 existing — 0.8 for 1. Here is the arithmetic that most coverage gets wrong, including my own first version of it.

What the issue actually does to a holder of one share

So the burden is not dilution — it is the cheque. A holder must find EGP 0.80 per share, about 12.5% of the current price, or sell the right to someone who will. The use of proceeds is undisclosed, and the company already approved and then postponed an equivalent raise in 2024.

The test that matters: does Madinet Masr put in its own EGP 105.8m share? A parent that awards its subsidiary nothing for a quarter and then declines to fund it has answered the question by itself.

04 / What I got wrong

Originally −23.
Revised to −20.

An independent rebuttal argued this entry was too harsh and belonged at −13 or −14. That is not supported by the evidence. But it was right on two specific points, and one of them was my error — so both are recorded here rather than quietly fixed.

  1. 01

    I called the rights issue “80% dilution at an 84% discount”. That was wrong.

    A pro-rata rights issue is value-neutral to anyone who subscribes or sells the right. This series had already learned that lesson on AMES and I repeated the mistake anyway. The entry now frames it correctly, as a cash call.

    My error
  2. 02

    I assumed the H1-2026 filing was figure-poor. It was not.

    It contains a gross profit of EGP 15,480k on revenue of EGP 120,126k — a 12.89% margin against 8.04%. That is a real improvement and I did not have it. Earnings quality moved from −8 to −6.

    +2 earned
  3. 03

    But the same document caps how far that can go.

    It is expressly unaudited, the result falls from +EGP 3,790k to a loss of EGP 6,006k, and operating cash flow widens from −EGP 5,908k to −EGP 32,747k — a 454% wider burn in the half the margin supposedly improved. Precedent: 9M-2025 showed +EGP 20,612k of gross profit, then Q4 booked −EGP 13,574k.

    Capped at +2
  4. 04

    Valuation moved too, for a reason I had underweighted.

    5× book is mid-pack for the EGX micro-cap contractor cohort (median ~4.5×) — legacy land at historic cost against a devalued pound. It cannot carry the harsh end of the band alone. −7 → −6.

    +1
