00 / Start here

MCQE in
normal English.

A cement plant in Qena that sold more cement at better prices while its biggest cost went down, holds more cash than debt, and has almost no shares available to buy.

  1. 01

    The profit nearly doubled, and it belongs to shareholders.

    Group profit rose 82.4% to EGP 1.430bn — but EGP 263.237m of that belongs to minorities. The correct figure for MCQE's own owners is EGP 1.166896bn, up 94.5%. Always take the owner line.

    +94.5%
  2. 02

    The margin came from the electricity bill.

    Sales rose 27.3% while electricity and power cost fell 4.3%, from EGP 1.523bn to EGP 1.457bn. As a share of sales that is 39.3% → 29.5%. The improvement is inside cost of sales, which is where a durable one lives.

    39.3% → 29.5%
  3. 03

    It has more cash than debt.

    Approximately EGP 1.52bn of net cash, a current ratio of 1.69×, EGP 238m of bank facilities and an unmodified limited review. This is the strongest balance sheet the series has scored.

    Net cash
  4. 04

    8.39%

01 / The bridge that matters

Sales up 27.3%.
The power bill down 4.3%.

When an Egyptian company's margin jumps, the first suspicion is always the same: a disposal, a revaluation, or an FX swing dressed as operating performance. The manufacturing note answers it directly.

H1 2026 versus H1 2025, from the signed consolidated statements
LineH1 2025H1 2026Change
RevenueEGP 3.878bnEGP 4.939bn+27.3%
Gross profitEGP 1.265bnEGP 2.016bn+59.4%
Gross margin32.6%40.8%+8.2 pts
Electricity & power costEGP 1.523bnEGP 1.457bn−4.3%
Power as a share of sales39.3%29.5%−9.8 pts
Group profit (all holders)—EGP 1.430bn+82.4%
Profit attributable to MCQE owners—EGP 1.166896bn+94.5%

What this proves, and what it does not. It proves the margin improvement sits inside cost of sales rather than coming from a property disposal or a one-off gain — which is the difference between a business getting better and an accounting event. It does not prove that any single energy project caused the saving; that link is not established and is not claimed here.

The cash test. FY2025 operating cash flow was EGP 3.054bn against EGP 2.558bn of total profit — profit converting to cash at better than 100%. That is why earnings quality scores +5. It is held at +5 rather than higher because H1 operating cash flow fell 52.4% as inventory, debtors and EGP 765.6m of tax absorbed cash.

And the acceleration is not unlimited. Q2 owner profit was still +43.5% year on year, but −19.9% sequentially. Recorded because the framework's growth pillar is about direction, not level.

What the correction changes. The 439,328 shares that had traded by the 11:16 quote were about 5.45% of that entire pool in a single morning. The original opportunity score is preserved at 9/13 for the record; re-running the same information set against the official float produces roughly 8/13 and smaller sizing — but it still qualifies a small model position after EGP 64.47m of value traded on 10 August.

Tradability −6. Around EGP 14m of value on a normal session and EGP 64m on a confirmation session is workable for small positions. The ±20% daily limit is the problem: it shows how quickly the exit disappears when eight holders control 91.61% of the register.

03 / Why there is so little stock

Two buyers had already
taken the supply.

The float did not shrink by accident. Two separate strategic accumulations ran through 2025, both disclosed at the time, and both are on the public record.

  1. 01

    Mohamed Ashraf Omar, from Ahly Capital.

    9.6m shares at EGP 23.25 on 27 March 2025 — EGP 223.2m. He later crossed 15.023% and reached 17.99%. That original block is marked at about EGP 2.339bn at the intraday price, roughly EGP 2.115bn above cost if it was retained.

    ≈10.5× on the block
  2. 02

    Misr Beni Suef Cement (MBSC).

    9.966m shares at EGP 30.089 on 22 May 2025 — EGP 299.870m, later reaching 12.99%. It explored buying up to 33%, but rejected the Competition Authority's conditional undertakings and the application was shelved. No current approval or renewed deal is established.

    Shelved at 33%

These facts explain scarce supply. They do not prove anything else. They do not establish that either holder caused the 11 August move, and they do not establish that anyone possessed non-public information. The identity of the ordinary-market buyers on 11 August is not established and is recorded as such.

04 / Publish the misses

The scanner was right.
I was late.

Rule six of this series says the misses get published with the original date attached. This one is a timing and execution miss, and it is recorded with no backdated profit and loss.

What the model saw, and what happened next
DateWhat the tape showedDecision
6 Aug 2026Post-filing session, heavy volume, but only 15.8% close strength after touching EGP 200Rejected — correctly
10 Aug 2026Close EGP 203, RV20 3.92×, 85.7% close strength, EGP 64.47m tradedPackage complete
11 Aug 2026Opened at EGP 203, inside the EGP 205 capWaited for more depth
11 Aug 2026Ran to EGP 243.60 by 11:16Missed the verified print

The scanner did not fail to confirm it. The 6 August rejection was the right call — a heavy-volume session that closes in the bottom 15.8% of its range is a rejection, not a breakout. The 10 August session completed the saved package on every gate. The portfolio workflow then waited for later depth instead of booking the verified opening print on the 11th, and that is the miss.

And the honest caveat. The saved targets of EGP 215.20 / 221.30 would ordinarily have forced an exit before EGP 243.60. Do not pretend the model would automatically have captured the full limit-up move. It would not have.

05 / The verdict

Cash at 243.60.
By a single point.

The price is timestamped deliberately: EGP 243.60 at 11:16:02 AM Cairo, a delayed intraday quote rather than a closing price. At 96m shares that is EGP 23.386bn of market value. Reconstructing owner profit from the primary consolidated statements gives TTM profit of EGP 2.600bn and EPS of EGP 27.09, so the P/E is 8.99× — not the stale vendor figure.

Valuation +2. 8.99× TTM owner earnings and roughly 5.3× EV/EBITDA are reasonable in absolute terms — but they are above ARCC, MBSC and SCEM after the +20% move, and price-to-book is 6.54× against parent book equity of EGP 3.578bn. This is the pillar that keeps the score at +20 rather than higher.

Earnings quality +5. The profit is operating: net other income was only EGP 20.7m. FY2025 operating cash flow of EGP 3.054bn against EGP 2.558bn of total profit. Held at +5 by the 52.4% fall in H1 cash conversion.

Balance sheet +8. Approximately EGP 1.52bn net cash, a 1.69× current ratio, EGP 238m of bank facilities and an unmodified limited review.

Governance +4. Timely H1 filing, unmodified review, named ownership and disclosed related parties, and no current enforcement finding located. Deduction for inconsistent investor-relations and vendor ownership data, and for extreme strategic-holder concentration.

What would flip it down: H2 margin normalisation, another period of weak cash conversion, or a rise above roughly EGP 270–300 without a matching earnings upgrade. What would strengthen it: a high-30s gross margin holding, working-capital cash reversing, and the filed other-shareholder pool rising above 10%.
