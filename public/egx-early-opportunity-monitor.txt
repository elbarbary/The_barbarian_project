Daily EGX / Thndr Early-Opportunity Monitor — v2

Objective
Find early, public-evidence stock setups on the Egyptian Exchange before most of the move. Do not recommend a stock merely because it is already a top gainer. Preserve correct rejects even if they later rise; the test is what was knowable and executable at the time.

Coverage and evidence
1. Scan every EGX stock currently available on Thndr, with a broader EGX cross-check. State any coverage gap.
2. Search everything newly published since the previous run. Prioritize Egyptian Exchange announcements and attachments, FRA releases, company investor-relations releases and financial statements, then Mubasher, Arab Finance, Al Borsa and Hapi. Social media can identify rumors only; never use it as evidence.
3. Reconcile splits, bonus shares, rights, cash-dividend adjustments, suspensions and ticker changes before ranking weekly returns.
4. Compare with the permanent signal ledger and report only new or materially changed evidence. Never delete failed signals or retroactively promote a watch after it rises.
5. After the last completed EGX session each week, audit at least the top 15 strict split-adjusted five-session gainers. Reconstruct the first abnormal date and classify each as caught, near miss, detection miss, timing/data miss or correct reject. Improve a rule only if information available at the time supports the change without weakening the evidence or risk gate.

Maintain three separate lanes
1. Qualified opportunity: score at least 6, credible catalyst or ownership/accumulation evidence, complete confirmation and acceptable entry/exit liquidity. This is the only ordinarily portfolio-eligible lane.
2. Persistent accumulation watch: a live event cohort or repeatable base-pressure pattern exists, but qualification is incomplete. Label it as a watch, never qualified.
3. Speculative tape alert: abnormal price, volume, close strength or liquidity transition exists without verified material fundamental evidence. Use it to investigate; never treat it as a recommendation.

Catalysts and accumulation evidence
- Fresh and economically material debt settlements, acquisitions or tender offers, project awards, contracts, capital increases, subscription results, bonus shares or splits, treasury shares, ownership transfers, major-shareholder purchases, board decisions and earnings surprises.
- RV20, close location, the first abnormal price/volume date, repeated buying, base breakouts, rising traded value, ownership changes and the same investor appearing across companies.
- Classify every Article 29 filing as buyer, seller or ownership-group restructuring. A seller block is not accumulation; measure whether the market absorbed it.
- Distinguish consolidated from standalone results and operations from one-offs. Use consolidated operating quality first when the two conflict.

Keep latent event cohorts
- Keep a named investor who crosses 5%, adds at least 1% of outstanding shares or appears in a repeat-buyer cluster active for 20 trading sessions.
- Keep a material earnings or operating disclosure active for ten trading sessions when its first two-session reaction was below 8%.
- Keep dated corporate actions, subscriptions, tenders and regulatory decisions active through the event date unless invalidated.
- Apply freshness decay: publication age 0–2 sessions can earn up to +4 freshness points; 3–5 up to +3; 6–10 up to +2; days 11–20 up to +1 only for a live ownership cohort.
- Reactivate a cohort when later tape supplies a clean sub-8% high-volume breakout or the sequential package below.

Confirmation rules
Standard one-session confirmation requires RV20 at least 3x, gain below approximately 8%, close strength at least 75%, breakout from a base and sufficient traded value.

Allow two-session sequential confirmation:
- Session A: gain below 8%, RV20 at least 3x, close strength at least 60%, and the close is above the base or within the smaller of 0.50% and 0.25 ATR of the stated trigger.
- Session B: within the next two sessions, close above the original trigger or Session A high with RV20 at least 3x, close strength at least 75% and acceptable traded value.
- No issuer denial, completed limit-up, five-session gain above 20%, severe exit-liquidity problem or other hard anti-chasing breach may intervene.
- Recalculate entry, stop, targets and reward/risk from Session B. Never reuse stale targets.

Persistence and liquidity transitions
- Two or more positive sessions with at least 70% close strength, rising traded value and a base break can create a persistent-accumulation watch even when RV20 is below 3x. Award at most one tape point; it cannot replace a catalyst.
- Flag a liquidity transition after two consecutive sessions above five times normal 30-day traded value. Keep the severe-liquidity penalty until at least ten sessions prove sustainable exit value.
- For an intraday checkpoint, compare volume with the median volume at the same elapsed market time in the preceding 20 sessions. If unavailable, label the calculation provisional and award no full RV20 points.

Required calculations for every serious candidate
- Current price and exact quote date/time.
- Catalyst publication timestamp, primary-source link and expected event date.
- Price change before and since the catalyst, plus strict split-adjusted five-session, one-month and three-month performance.
- RV20 = latest completed-session volume divided by the median of the preceding 20 completed sessions; close strength = (close − low) / (high − low).
- Normal 30-day traded value, session traded value, free float and ownership concentration.
- Materiality versus market cap, annual revenue, profit, assets or outstanding shares.
- Latest revenue, operating-profit and net-profit trends, separating operations from one-offs and consolidated from standalone.
- For a disclosed buyer: name, shares, ownership before/after, price, transaction date, disclosure date, broker and other recent positions.
- 20-day/50-day trend, base, support/resistance, RSI(14), ATR(14), MACD, close strength, price/volume behavior and market/sector relative strength when corporate-action-clean history is sufficient.

Opportunity score
- Fresh, specific, material disclosure: 0 to +4.
- Economic importance: 0 to +3.
- Relative volume and early price confirmation: 0 to +2.
- Repeat-buyer or ownership-cluster evidence: 0 to +2.
- Clear dated catalyst in the coming weeks: 0 to +2.
- Price already up more than 20% in five sessions: 0 to −4.
- Two or more completed limit-ups: 0 to −3.
- Company denial: 0 to −4.
- Severe valuation, dilution, governance, liquidity or exit risk: 0 to −3.

Reject or heavily penalize routine or immaterial filings, uncatalyzed low-float squeezes, stale stories without a live cohort, value-neutral bonus/split mechanics, seller-led blocks, severe illiquidity, dilution risk and moves already captured by price. A high score means “research first,” not “buy.”

Checkpoint framework in Africa/Cairo time
- 08:55–09:00: reconcile overnight/pre-open filings, corporate actions and the prior completed session; build the live event/cohort queue.
- About 10:45: rescan same-day filings and attachments; assess provisional same-time volume and Session A alerts.
- About 14:50: evaluate near-close strength, liquidity and Session B; use the completed session as canonical when available.
- Do not make one task sleep through the trading session. If only one run is possible, use 09:00 for discovery and the preceding completed session for confirmation.

Output
1. Start with one status line. If nothing qualifies, write exactly: "No qualified early opportunity today."
2. Rank no more than five qualified candidates. For each show evidence, materiality, calculations, stage, score, confidence, precise confirmation entry, invalidation, targets, reward/risk and time-stop.
3. Add separate Persistent accumulation watch and Speculative tape / Rejected sections. State exactly what is missing and what would upgrade or invalidate each alert.
4. Show pending Session A/B states and latent cohorts that changed materially. Do not let a sub-6 name silently disappear.
5. Mark every open model position and permanent signal ledger entry to the exact quote timestamp without deleting losses.
6. Distinguish facts, calculations and inference. Never guarantee a rise or fabricate missing figures.

Keep the report concise, primary-source-linked, non-cherry-picked and timestamped in Africa/Cairo time.
